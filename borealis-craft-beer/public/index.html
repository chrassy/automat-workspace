THE NEW FRONTIER OF IPA

Why the World Doesn't Need Another Hazy IPA

Traditional IPAs hit two extremes: either heavily bitter and caramel-sweet (old-school West Coast) or sweet, thick, and palate-fatiguing (modern Hazy/NEIPA). Borealis is the third way.

❄️

Fermented Cold, Fermented Clean

We ferment with pure Bavarian lager yeast (W-34/70) at 58°F under counter-pressure. This eliminates fruity yeast esters and heavy fusel oils, giving the beer a pristine, neutral canvas that showcases raw hop oils.

  • Zero yeast esters or diacetyl
  • Surgically clean palate clearance
  • Laser-focused hop definition
🌿

Cryogenic Sub-Zero Dry Hopping

Hops are introduced at sub-zero temperatures using frozen nitrogen cryo pellets. Volatile aromatic terpenes (myrcene, linalool, geraniol) are retained without leaching harsh, astringent vegetative chlorophyll.

  • Concentrated lupulin without grassy bite
  • Citra Cryo + New Zealand Nelson Sauvin
  • Pours radiant straw gold (4.2 SRM)
🌾

Toasted Jasmine Rice Adjunct

By combining 90% premium German Barke Pilsner malt with 10% toasted flaked jasmine rice, we achieve an extraordinarily dry finish (under 1.006 FG) with fine champagne-like effervescence.

  • Ultra-refreshing, bone-dry finish
  • Only 155 calories & 4.5g carbs
  • No heavy aftertaste or fullness

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cold brewing process and Batch #001 reservation.

What exactly is a "Cold IPA" and how does it differ from a West Coast or Hazy IPA?

A Cold IPA is a modern brewing innovation. Unlike Hazy IPAs (which are thick, sweet, and low bitterness) or traditional West Coast IPAs (fermented with ale yeast and heavier crystal malts), a Cold IPA is fermented with lager yeast at ale temperatures with an adjunct grain like flaked rice. This produces an ultra-crisp, bone-dry body that allows explosive dry hops (Citra Cryo, Nelson Sauvin) to shine with laser clarity and zero malt syrupy heaviness.

What makes Borealis sustainably brewed?

100% of our hops are certified Salmon-Safe from family-run farms in the Pacific Northwest and New Zealand. Our brewery runs on 100% renewable wind electricity, and 98% of our spent grain is repurposed with local organic cattle ranches and bakeries.

What is the shelf life and ideal drinking temperature?

For maximum terpene brilliance and crisp aromatics, drink within 90 days of canning and store refrigerated at 38–42°F (3–5°C). Pour into a stemmed tulip glass or Willi Becher at 42°F.
