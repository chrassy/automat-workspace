FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Switching

Does PulseGym work with my existing turnstiles and door locks? ▼
Yes! PulseGym is hardware-agnostic. Our relay gateway connects effortlessly to Kisi, Paxton, HID, dormakaba, and generic 12V/24V magnetic strike and turnstile relays. Members can unlock doors via Apple Wallet NFC, Android Bluetooth, RFID key fobs, or barcode cards.
How difficult is it to migrate from Mindbody or Glofox? ▼
Our Concierge Migration Team handles the entire process for you in under 48 hours. We export your active members, payment credentials (via PCI-compliant token transfer so members don't have to re-enter their cards), class histories, and waivers with zero downtime.
How does the automated dunning recover failed payments? ▼
When a monthly card charge declines, PulseGym instantly activates an intelligent retry sequence at optimal banking intervals. If still declined, the member receives a gentle, white-labeled SMS with a secure 1-click Apple Pay or card update link. 74% of declined payments are resolved within 48 hours without staff intervention.
Can we have our own gym logo on the mobile app? ▼
Yes! On our Growth and Scale plans, your mobile app is published directly under your gym's brand name in the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, customized with your colors, logo, and community board.
Are there long-term contracts or cancellation fees? ▼
Never. All PulseGym plans are month-to-month by default with no long-term contracts. If you choose our discounted annual plan, you are covered by a 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee.
